## 👋 About Me

<img align="right" src="https://github.com/CathyKernel.png" width="160" alt="Cathy's avatar" />

Hey! I'm **Cathy** — and yes, the *Kernel* in **CathyKernel** is deliberate: I'm drawn to the core of things, whether that's operating-system kernels, kernel methods in machine learning, or the elegant heart of a mathematical proof.

- 🔭 **Right now I'm asking how machines remember** — probing whether factual recall in small language models comes down to *decodability* or *causality* (that's my [FactLens](https://github.com/CathyKernel/FactLens-Decodability-vs.-Causality-of-Factual-Recall-in-Small-Language-Models) research project)
- 📈 **Quant-curious** — I love bending probability and statistics into working tools, from RCT analysis to a live-updating US equity quant terminal
- 🧩 **Puzzles become programs** — N-Queens, Sudoku, a Rubik's Cube solver, even a hand of Texas Hold'em
- 🌌 **Code is my canvas** — I once brought Van Gogh's *Starry Night* to life with pure code
- 🎵 **My daily equation:** math for logic, music for rhythm, and code to bring both to life
